What you will do

Three focused responsibilities. You will follow clear guidelines and be trained on the tools before you start.

AI response rating

Read AI-generated answers and score them for accuracy, helpfulness, clarity and tone, then compare alternatives side by side.

Spam identification

Review messages and content batches and mark what is spam, misleading or low quality so it can be filtered out.

AI guardrails

Check that AI responses stay safe and appropriate, and flag outputs that break the safety rules you are given.
